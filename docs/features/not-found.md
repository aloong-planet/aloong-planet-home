# Not-found page

## Overview
A visitor who follows a broken link or mistypes an address needs to know the page is not there and how to get back. Any address the site does not have shows a not-found page with a puzzled golden retriever and two ways back.

## Capabilities
- See the not-found page at any address the site does not have, including the address of a removed post: the top navigation, a puzzled golden retriever with a tilted head, "404", "这一页走丢了", and "小狗找了一圈，没找到这个地址。可能是链接打错了，或者页面已经搬走。".
- Go back with "回首页" to the homepage or "看看博客" to the Blog page.
- Reach Apps, Agents, Blog, About, or GitHub from the top navigation, including the [Apps and Agents menus](nav-menus.md); no section is marked as the current one.
- See the whole page in the first screen of a phone or a desktop window.

## Boundaries and non-goals
- Opening the not-found page does not count as a visit and shows no visitor number.
- The page does not show the requested address, suggest posts, or redirect moved posts.
- The dog does not wag on this page.
- Without scripting, the top navigation does not appear; the dog, text, and buttons still work.
- The page comes in one light appearance and one language.

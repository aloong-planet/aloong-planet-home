# Apps and Agents menus

## Overview
A visitor on any page can open one of Aloong's apps, skills or plugins straight from the top navigation, without scrolling the homepage first. The homepage lists the skills and plugins in its Agents section and shows a card for every app in its Apps section.

## Capabilities
- See the top navigation read Apps, Agents, Blog and About on the homepage, the Blog page, every post and the not-found page.
- Rest the pointer on Apps or Agents to open a menu under it that lists each work by name with a short description. Apps lists the apps; Agents lists the skills under 「Skills」 and the plugins under 「Plugins」.
- Choose an app to open its website in the same tab, or a skill or plugin to open its GitHub repository in a new tab.
- Tab onto Apps or Agents to open its menu, and Tab on through its items to the next tab. Press Escape, or select the tab itself, to hide the menu until the pointer and keyboard focus have both left it.
- Select Apps or Agents to go to that section of the homepage.
- Browse the Agents section on the homepage: the skills and plugins in two lists side by side, each with what it does, opening its GitHub repository in a new tab. Narrow screens stack the two lists.

## Boundaries and non-goals
- Menus open only in windows wider than 700px with a pointer that can hover. On touch screens and narrow windows, Apps and Agents go straight to their homepage sections, which list the same works.
- While the pointer rests on one menu, the other stays closed even when keyboard focus is in it. It opens again when the pointer leaves, if focus is still on it.
- Without scripting, the top navigation and its menus do not appear; the homepage's Apps and Agents sections still show.
- Menu items have no icons or screenshots. The app websites do not carry these menus.
- The site has one language: JustSnap appears under its Chinese name, 随译.

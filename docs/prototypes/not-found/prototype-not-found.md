## Question
Does the page shown for an address the site does not have tell visitors they are lost and get them back to the homepage or the Blog?

## Structure basis
The layout is the direction the user confirmed among three: a centered single column with the sitting dog on top, then "404", the heading, the explanation and two buttons, 回首页 and 看看博客. The top navigation is the shared header of the other pages; there is no footer. The dog is the homepage's sitting golden retriever drawn with a puzzled, searching face and a tilted head, shown at the homepage dog's displayed size, 160 pixels. The page shows no visitor number. The browser tab title in the product is 这一页走丢了 · Aloong Planet; in the gallery the app name comes from the gallery's tab label instead.

The two buttons are a new style: the site has no button yet, only underlined text links.

Per the project capabilities, i18n and theming are disabled; both are skipped. The page has no icons.

## Uncovered dimensions
Only the fixed light appearance and the single existing language are covered. The prototype gallery is review infrastructure, not part of the design.
